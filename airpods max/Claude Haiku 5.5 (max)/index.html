AirPods Max

Active Noise Cancellation, a knit canopy and memory-foam cushions, shown one part at a time.

Finish

Five finishes.

Silver, Space Gray, Sky Blue, Pink and Green. The cups, canopy, cushions and case change color together.

Controls

Two controls on the right cup.

The Digital Crown and the Noise Control button sit on the outer edge of the right ear cup.

Comfort

Knit canopy, knit cushions.

A breathable canopy spreads the weight across the top of your head. Each cushion is knit over memory foam, and the inside of each cup is marked L or R.

Magnetic cushions

They lift off and snap back.

Each cushion attaches with magnets. Lift one away to see the perforated driver housing and the magnet ring, then press it home.

Engineering

Layer by layer.

Anodized shell, H1 chip and sensors, a 40 mm driver with a ring magnet, the acoustic baffle, and the knit cushion.
