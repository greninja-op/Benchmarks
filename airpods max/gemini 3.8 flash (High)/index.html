The canopy spanning the stainless-steel headband frame is made from an acoustically transparent knit mesh, distributing weight evenly to reduce on-head pressure. Telescoping arms extend smoothly and stay right where you set them.

  • Custom 3D diamond-knit textile with pillow-soft memory foam

Modular Architecture

Acoustically sealed.
Magnetically effortless.

Each ear cushion attaches via precision neodymium ring magnets. Pull to remove, or let them snap seamlessly back into the perforated acoustic baffle plate with a clean mechanical click.

  • 36-aperture perforated acoustic baffle engineered for clean airflow
  • Swap contrasting cushion colorways for a personalized aesthetic

Acoustic Engineering

40mm dynamic driver.
Dual H1 processors.

Equipped with a custom dual-neodymium ring magnet motor modeled after high-end floor-standing speakers. It minimizes total harmonic distortion to less than 1% across the entire audible range—even at maximum volume.

  • Apple H1 chip in each cup running 9 billion operations per second
  • Real-time Adaptive EQ adjusts sound to the bespoke fit of your ear cushions

Intelligent Preservation

A case with staying power.

When stored in their soft, slim Smart Case, AirPods Max swivel flat and instantly enter an ultra-low-power state that preserves battery charge for weeks.
